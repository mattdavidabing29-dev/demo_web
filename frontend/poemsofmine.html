Poems of Mine

A Cruel Paradox

The paradox is cruel. I keep searching for something meaningful to bring, forgetting that perhaps the truest thing—

I have the wish to share a moment with you. Even when neither of us has words worthy of the space between us,

I find comfort in the silence because, at least for a while, I get to see you from a shorter distance.

The Foolish Prince

A fool can yearn for as long as certainty remains. Such is the folly of the Prince.

But do not mistake this Prince's foolishness for weakness

When certainty fades and uncertainty takes its throne, he does not surrender to doubt. Instead,

he wages wars not against kingdoms, but against silence itself. He will endure every hardship, bear every painful truth, and seek every path that leads to clarity,

he wages wars not against kingdoms, but against silence itself. He will endure every hardship, bear every painful truth, and seek every path that leads to clarity,

for he would rather be wounded by the truth than be imprisoned by uncertainty. Whether it is the painful truth or the gentle assurance of the queen he longs for,

he Prince will pursue it without hesitation, for certainty, however cruel or kind, is kinder than endless wondering.
